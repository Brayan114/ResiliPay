# 🚀 How to Publish ResiliPay on Zenodo & Get an Instant DOI

**Zenodo** (operated by CERN and OpenAIRE) is the premier open-access research repository. Publishing on Zenodo provides you with an **official, permanent, citable DOI (Digital Object Identifier)** recognized worldwide and fully accepted by conferences like **ACM COMPASS**.

---

## ⏱️ Step-by-Step Instructions (Takes ~3 Minutes)

### Step 1: Create or Log in to Zenodo
1. Navigate to **[https://zenodo.org](https://zenodo.org)**.
2. Log in using your email, **GitHub account**, or **ORCID**. (Logging in with GitHub is fastest!).

---

### Step 2: Start a New Upload
1. Click the green **"New Upload"** button at the top right (or go to [https://zenodo.org/deposit/new](https://zenodo.org/deposit/new)).

---

### Step 3: Upload Your Files
Upload the following files from your local project:
1. **The Compiled PDF** (compiled from `paper/main.tex` or `paper/acm_compass/main.tex`).
2. *(Optional but highly recommended)*: The source zip containing `paper/` and the benchmark results (`benchmarks/`).

---

---

### Step 5: Reserve and Publish
1. Click **"Reserve DOI"** at the top of the form if you want to preview the exact DOI number before publishing.
2. Click **"Publish"**.

🎉 **You now have a permanent, citable academic DOI!**  
Your DOI will look like `10.5281/zenodo.XXXXXXX` and can be cited in CVs, research proposals, grant applications, and on your GitHub repository badge.
